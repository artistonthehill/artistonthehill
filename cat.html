Good to know

Each price includes VAT, framing, delivery and a certificate of provenance.

Any of these can be paid for gradually through the Waiting Room: a monthly standing order from your own bank, with no interest and no credit check. The painting comes home with you, framed, once the first payment is in.

I am also available for commissions: a place that matters to you, a view from a window, a house, an occasion.

Message Tony

I paint in oils, mostly outdoors and on the spot. I was the in-house artist for the first two series of Sky Arts Portrait Artist of the Year, and have exhibited with the ROI and RSMA at the Mall Galleries in London.
