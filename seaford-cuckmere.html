Your painting comes with a permanent record

Every painting I sell carries a TOAT — The Original Art Trust — certification. A physical aluminium token is attached to the back of the painting, linked to a permanent Ethereum blockchain ownership record. It travels with the work forever: through resales, gifts, inheritances. Your name goes on it from the moment we agree the sale.
